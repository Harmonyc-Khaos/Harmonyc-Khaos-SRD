# Appendix B: Karma

Karma is an universal law of cause and effect where your actions, words, and intents shape your future experiences.

In Haros, Karma was subconsciouly created by the All-Mother as a cosmic force that affects how each being's Mana flows and manifests. This cosmic force exists as a dichotomy between what mortals would describe as "Good" and "Evil", and one's behaviors would push them to be described as either of those.

Deities' nourishment on the power of belief granted to them by their followers may be affected if a large number of creatures whose Karma is the opposite of the one that deity favors, being able to either purify or corrupt them.

The Karma System is part of your character and it tracks the aforementioned behaviors on a numerical scale which may affect how certain magical effects behave within other in-world consequences.

A dweller's Karma whose value exceeds positive 150 becomes **Virtue**{: .positive-karma} and if the value exceeds negative 150, it becomes **Sin**{: .negative-karma}. If your Karma value sits between both aforementioned boundaries, then your Karma is **Neutral**{: .neutral-karma}.

Behaviors a character follows may be rewarded (or punished, in case that their behavior goes against their established character) by the addition or removal of Karma points by the Game Master, though the initial value should be decided by both the Game Master and the character's player, based on a multitude of factors that make up said character, like per example, the description and backstory of said character or the environment that character is native to. It should be noted that if a character follows a behavior that is already congruent with what is expected of them then that there is no need to alter their Karma value.

Below is a chart with a number of example behaviors and what their associated value would be.

| Behavior | Karma Value |
|---|---|
| Donating to the destitute | **+5**{: .positive-karma} |
| Saving an innocent life | **+15**{: .positive-karma} |
| Kill in self-defense | **0**{: .neutral-karma} |
| Lying for personal gain | **-10**{: .negative-karma} |
| Theft | **-20**{: .negative-karma} |
| Robbery | **-30**{: .negative-karma} |
| Murder of an innocent | **-50**{: .negative-karma} |
