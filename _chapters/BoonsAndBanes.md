### Heaven and Hell (Boon & Bane)

(WIP)

Virtue and Sin crests

Depending on your **Karma** value your crest either becomes a Virtue or Sin crest.

Charity - Greed
Chastity - Lust
Diligence - Sloth
Humility - Pride
Kindness - Envy
Patience - Wrath
Temperance - Gluttony

Taking this feature counts as taking both 1 Boon and 1 Bane.

### Skewered Fate (Boon)

All your D20 Rolls become 2d10 instead.

Taking this feature counts as taking 2 Boons.

### Vow to Mane (Bane)

(WIP)

You cannot use any effect that utilizes Mana without spending a ⬢ token first during each of your turns. Those effects then get powered up.


### Chaos Wave (Bane)

(WIP)

You must expend an additional ⬢ token to perform Emission-based effects. If you do so, your movement is then reduced by half for that turn and the range of all Emission-based effects are increased by 4.5 m.

To take this feature you must have at least **Novice** proficiency in Emission.

### Hard Mode (Bane)

Your character's starting Attribute values are all reduced by 1.
